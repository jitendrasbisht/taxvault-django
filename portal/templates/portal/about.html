About TaxVault

Jitendra Singh, PMP®

Founder, TaxVault

Jitendra spent 17+ years in financial services — most recently managing Risk & Control at American Express — building automation and BI systems that turn manual processes into ones that just run.

Every tax season, CA firms lose hours to the same problem: hundreds of client documents landing in a folder, with no easy way to tell who's sent what. TaxVault solves exactly that — point it at a folder of mixed documents and it reads, matches, and files each one to the right client automatically, flagging anything it can't confidently place for a quick manual check. At any moment, the firm knows exactly who's Ready for ITR and who's still missing what. It doesn't file returns — its job ends the moment a client's paperwork is complete.
